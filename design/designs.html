AVX-CORE-10 — DATASHEETREV 2026.07 · SUPERSEDES 2026.05

AVX-CORE-10

Governed Market Research Signal Processor

Converts market data, news, social media, and technical signals into governed research outputs with confidence scores, source attribution, trace IDs, and risk-gated evaluation.

1 Features

  • 10 independent signal modules — news, chart, metrics and others, each scored 0–1
  • Composite confidence output with per-source attribution
  • Integrated Governor risk gate — hard limits, cooldowns, compliance emission
  • Full trace bus — every output resolves to its exact pipeline run via trace_id
  • 24/7 runtime supervision (EYE module) with immutable audit logs
  • Structured JSON output — machine-readable, workflow-ready
  • advice pin permanently LOW — outputs are research, never financial advice

2 Description

The AVX-CORE-10 is a modular financial analysis pipeline for researchers, systematic quant teams, and institutional workflows. Unlike black-box devices, every internal net is exposed for inspection: atomic signals, composition logic, and governance decisions are all readable from the trace bus.

The device will not emit while the Governor gate is held; rejected cycles are logged, never silently discarded.
